leg
/obj/item/organ/cyberimp/leg

Leg-mounted cyberimplants.

Legs have no tool slots, so these augments do not deploy items: they are permanent passive/activated upgrades until surgically removed. Every leg implant is installed as a pair and occupies both leg slots at once.

Vars

occupies_both_legsWhile implanted, this augment binds both leg slots and can be extracted from either leg.
